## 📌 About the Project

This project is a **fictional business case** about **Eniac**, a European technology company considering an expansion into the Brazilian market.

Eniac is evaluating a **three-year partnership with Magist**, a Brazilian e-commerce service provider. Our task is to analyze Magist's e-commerce data and determine whether Magist is a suitable partner, with a particular focus on **selling and distributing technology products** in Brazil.

---

## 🎯 Business Question

> ### Should Eniac sign a three-year partnership with Magist?

To answer this, we look at Magist's data from four business perspectives:

| # | Perspective | Key Questions |
|:-:|-------------|---------------|
| 1️⃣ | **📦 Product Portfolio** | Which product categories generate the highest revenue? Which technology-related categories perform particularly well? |
| 2️⃣ | **🚚 Sellers & Delivery** | How long do sellers take to deliver on average? Which sellers have enough order volume to be considered reliable? How do tech sellers compare? |
| 3️⃣ | **🗺️ Geographic Reach** | Which states generate the most orders and revenue? Which cities are the most important sales markets? |
| 4️⃣ | **⭐ Reliability & Satisfaction** | What percentage of orders arrive on time? What is the average revenue per order? Do late deliveries affect customer satisfaction? |

## 📊 Analysis & Methodology

<details open>
<summary><b>📦 Product Performance</b></summary>
<br>

We identify the product categories generating the highest revenue and evaluate how technology-related products perform.

</details>

<details open>
<summary><b>🚚 Seller & Delivery Performance</b></summary>
<br>

We analyze seller delivery times and identify sellers with a significant number of technology-related orders.

> 💡 **Threshold:** Only sellers with **at least 100 relevant orders** are compared, to ensure a meaningful basis for comparison.

</details>

<details open>
<summary><b>🗺️ Geographic Performance</b></summary>
<br>

We analyze orders and revenue by Brazilian **state** and **city** to identify Magist's strongest markets.

</details>

<details open>
<summary><b>⭐ Customer Satisfaction</b></summary>
<br>

We compare customer reviews for orders **delivered on time** versus **delivered late** to determine whether delivery performance affects satisfaction.

</details>

---

## 📈 Project Results

The results are used to evaluate whether Magist has the necessary:

- 🌍 **Market reach**
- 💻 **Technology product potential**
- 🤝 **Seller network**
- ⏱️ **Delivery performance**

to support Eniac's expansion into Brazil. The final recommendation on the three-year partnership is based on the **combined findings** and is presented in the final presentation.
