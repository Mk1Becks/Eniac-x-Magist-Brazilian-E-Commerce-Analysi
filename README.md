Eniac x Magist: Brazilian E-Commerce Analysis
📌 About the Project

This project is a fictional business case about Eniac, a European technology company considering an expansion into the Brazilian market.

Eniac is considering a three-year partnership with Magist, a Brazilian e-commerce service provider. Our task is to analyze Magist's e-commerce data and determine whether Magist would be a suitable partner for Eniac.

The analysis focuses particularly on Magist's suitability for selling and distributing technology products in the Brazilian market.

🎯 Business Question

Should Eniac sign a three-year partnership with Magist?

To answer this question, we analyze Magist's data from different business perspectives.

Key Business Questions

1. What are Magist's strongest product categories?

Which product categories generate the highest revenue?

Which technology-related categories perform particularly well?

2. Which sellers provide fast and reliable deliveries?

How long do sellers take to deliver orders on average?

Which sellers have sufficient order volume to be considered reliable?

How does delivery performance compare among sellers of technology products?

3. Where is Magist most successful in Brazil?

Which states generate the most orders and revenue?

Which cities are the most important sales markets?

4. How reliable are Magist's sellers?

What percentage of orders are delivered on time?

What is the average revenue per order?

Do late deliveries have an impact on customer satisfaction?

These questions help us evaluate Magist's product portfolio, seller network, delivery performance, customer satisfaction and geographical reach.

📊 Analysis & Results

Our analysis focuses on the following areas:

Product Performance

We identify the product categories generating the highest revenue and evaluate the performance of technology-related products.

Seller & Delivery Performance

We analyze seller delivery times and identify sellers with a significant number of technology-related orders. Sellers with at least 100 relevant orders are considered to provide a more meaningful basis for comparison.

Geographic Performance

We analyze orders and revenue by Brazilian state and city to identify Magist's strongest geographical markets.

Customer Satisfaction

We compare customer reviews for orders delivered on time versus orders delivered late to determine whether delivery performance affects customer satisfaction.

📈 Project Results

The results of these analyses are used to evaluate whether Magist has the necessary market reach, technology product potential, seller network and delivery performance to support Eniac's expansion into Brazil.

The final recommendation regarding the three-year partnership is presented based on the combined findings.
